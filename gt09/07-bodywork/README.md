# 07 — Bodywork (GT-09 V1, later phase)

**Not started. Do not start before Project 1's frame is signed off.**

When it's time, V1 bodywork is about *appearance and feasibility*, not aero:
- Nose cone, sidepods and rear-wing endplates as separate shells over a simple subframe, so they can
  be made (and remade) without touching the chassis.
- Materials in order of friendliness: **foam board / XPS carved and skinned**, then **fibreglass** only
  once you've practised on flat coupons.
- Every panel must come off with the car on the trailer, and nothing may block getting the driver out.

Design targets carried from the brief: low and wide, aggressive nose, sidepods, rear wing, halo-style
cockpit surround, exposed wheels. Visually convincing *without pretending to be a real F1 car*.
