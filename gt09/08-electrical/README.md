# 08 — Electrical (later phase)

**Not started.** For V1 (display) the electrical scope is deliberately tiny and 12 V only:
- Battery + master switch, so nothing drains or shorts on the trailer.
- A small harness for: brake/tail light (if fitted), and optional show lighting for the entrance.
- Everything fused, everything in loom, a printed wiring diagram kept in this folder.

If V2 adds a low-power drivetrain, electrical grows an E-stop, and its design and inspection join the
[03-01 safety review](../03-engineering/03-01-safety-review.md) list. No electrical work is "decorative"
enough to skip fusing.
