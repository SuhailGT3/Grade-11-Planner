# 10 — The matric entrance: run-of-show (later phase)

**Not started, and it stays a plan until the school approves it in writing.** The entrance is a short,
calm, cinematic moment — never a stunt. The car may arrive on a trailer even if it is driveable.

## The sequence (~90 seconds)

1. GT-09 arrives at an **approved** spot; engine/transport off; wheels chocked.
2. You step out in the full GT-09 racing suit.
3. Two pit crew approach in matching team shirts/gloves.
4. They remove the outer racing suit.
5. Revealed: your normal matric outfit underneath.
6. Crew take the suit; you walk into school. Confident, unhurried, done.

## Preconditions (all must be true)

- School / principal approval **before** any detail work; agree the location, time and that the car is
  static or trailered.
- If the car is driven *at all*, it has passed [03-01](../03-engineering/03-01-safety-review.md) in
  full, is driven at walking speed on private/controlled ground to position, never on a public road.
- Two crew rehearsed so the reveal is one smooth take; a third person on phone for photos/video.
- A wet-weather and a "car won't start / won't arrive" fallback (the suit reveal can happen beside the
  trailered car — the moment is the suit, the car is the set).

## Why this ordering matters

The entrance is the *reward*, not the *goal*. It only works if the year of learning, CAD and
fabrication behind it is real — that's the story everyone will tell afterwards. Build the year first;
the ninety seconds take care of themselves.
